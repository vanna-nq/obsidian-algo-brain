## Relationship semantics
- **Shared formalism:** methods share a mathematical structure or measurement, but need not support the same empirical claim.
- **Model comparison:** alternate mechanisms for the same target; use them as competing baselines.
- **Proposed synthesis:** a new hypothesis *we* are proposing, **not** a result claimed by either paper.
- **Validation safeguard:** methods for testing or qualifying claims; not alpha discoveries.
