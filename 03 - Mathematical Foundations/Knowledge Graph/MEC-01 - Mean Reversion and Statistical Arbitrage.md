# MEC-01 — Mean Reversion and Statistical Arbitrage

**Concept note only —** the links below organize mathematical overlap across source-screened research papers; they do **not** establish that the mechanisms apply profitably to current markets.

## Mechanism family
Spread stationarity, first-passage thresholds, contrarian reversals and statistical-arbitrage nulls are related but not identical models.

## Possible quantitative research use (inference)
A normalized spread can converge only if stationarity survives changing fundamentals and all-in trading costs.

## Identification and falsification safeguards
Prove cointegration/pair stability with train-only fitting; compare against random-pair and conditional liquidity controls.
