# MEC-07 — Variance Risk Premia and Derivative Hedging

**Concept note only —** the links below organize mathematical overlap across source-screened research papers; they do **not** establish that the mechanisms apply profitably to current markets.

## Mechanism family
Risk-neutral implied variance, realized variance, volatility risk compensation and hedge trading frictions.

## Possible quantitative research use (inference)
Variance risk premium and conditional volatility are distinct; an option surface is generally necessary to replicate implied quantities.

## Identification and falsification safeguards
Require timestamped options; distinguish realized variance forecast, risk premium and hedge-execution objectives.
