# MEC-08 — Intraday Seasonality and Price Range Formation

**Concept note only —** the links below organize mathematical overlap across source-screened research papers; they do **not** establish that the mechanisms apply profitably to current markets.

## Mechanism family

## Possible quantitative research use (inference)
