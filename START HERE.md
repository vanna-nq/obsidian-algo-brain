# Algo Brain — Quantitative Trading Research

This vault separates outside claims, untested hypotheses, executed results, and validated models.

## Workflow
1. Save new papers in [[00 - Research Inbox/README|Research Inbox]].
2. Create one canonical review per paper in [[01 - Paper Library/README|Paper Library]] using [[Templates/Paper Note Template|Paper Note Template]].
3. Link it to one or more [[02 - Trading Methodologies/README|Trading Methodologies]].
4. Form a falsifiable hypothesis in [[05 - Strategy Hypotheses/README|Strategy Hypotheses]].
5. Run reproducible experiments in [[06 - Experiments and Backtests/README|Experiments and Backtests]].
6. Promote only defensible evidence to [[07 - Validated Models/README|Validated Models]], and preserve failures in [[10 - Failed and Rejected Ideas/README|Failed and Rejected Ideas]].

## Methodology branches
Statistical Arbitrage · Trend and Momentum · Market Microstructure · Machine Learning · Deep Learning · Information Theory · Volatility and Derivatives · Regime Detection · Mean Reversion · Optimization and Physics · Intraday Price Structure · Cross-Asset and Macro.

## Research integrity
- Attribute each paper to its author; link to the source; respect its licensing.
- ResearchGate is a distribution platform, not a quality-control standard. Review venue, methods and evidence for every item.
- Do not equate reported in-sample performance with live profitability.
- Preserve unsuccessful experiments and track data provenance, fill assumptions, and research selection bias.
- Sync these Markdown files back into Obsidian through the configured GitHub plugin.
