# PAPER-036 — Carry

**Authors:** Ralph S. J. Koijen; Tobias J. Moskowitz; Lasse Heje Pedersen; Evert B. Vrugt  
**Publication:** Journal of Financial Economics 127(2), 197–225 (2018)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2017.11.002  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Generalizes ex-ante carry as predictable return earned when asset prices do not change, distinct from expected price appreciation.

## Source-supported result or scope
Authors report that carry predicts cross-sectional and time-series returns in global equity, bond, currency, commodity, credit and option markets.

## Risks and limitations
Carry exposure may embed crash, liquidity, funding and volatility risk; inconsistent carry definitions across assets create spurious comparisons.

## Required data for replication
Point-in-time forwards, yield curves, dividend yields, funding/borrow curves, contract specifications and executable roll prices.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Build ex-ante asset-specific carry signals with an identical funding convention and compare realized net carry to matched volatility and risk-factor benchmarks.
