## Mechanism / mathematical idea
Introduces maximum-entropy inference based on partial constraints, connecting Shannon entropy, partition functions and statistical mechanics.

## Author-reported finding or theoretical scope
Foundational derivation of distributions consistent with constrained information; no financial market backtest.

## Required data / feasibility gate
Observable moment constraints, proper probability support, finite-sample estimation and explicitly measured held-out predictive likelihood.

## Critique / potential failure
A maximum-entropy assumption does not recover unobserved market states or make prices predictable; constraints can be biased and nonstationary.

## Falsifiable follow-up (proposal only)
Benchmark a maximum-entropy regime/return distribution against Gaussian and empirical nonparametric forecasts on held-out likelihood and tail calibration.
