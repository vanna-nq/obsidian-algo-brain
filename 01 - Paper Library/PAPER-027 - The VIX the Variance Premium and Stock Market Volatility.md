# PAPER-027 — The VIX the Variance Premium and Stock Market Volatility

**Authors:** Geert Bekaert; Marie Hoerova  
**Publication:** Journal of Econometrics 183(2), 181–192 (2014)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2014.05.008  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Decomposes squared VIX into conditional stock-return variance and variance premium under explicitly estimated volatility forecasting assumptions.

## Source-supported result or scope
Authors report differential predictive links: variance premium relates to equity returns while expected variance relates more to activity and instability.

## Risks and limitations
VIX² is not a direct gamma measure; variance premium depends on forecast model; forward-looking realized targets and overlapping windows can leak.

## Required data for replication
VIX, real-time forecast inputs, intraday realized SPX variance, release timing and target-horizon alignment.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Compare variance premium and raw VIX as future-volatility and return regime predictors in chronologically held-out data.
