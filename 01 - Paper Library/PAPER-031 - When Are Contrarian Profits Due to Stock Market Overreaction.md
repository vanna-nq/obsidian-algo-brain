# PAPER-031 — When Are Contrarian Profits Due to Stock Market Overreaction

**Authors:** Andrew W. Lo; A. Craig MacKinlay  
**Publication:** Review of Financial Studies 3(2), 175–205 (1990)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/3.2.175  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Demonstrates that cross-stock lead-lag structure may create contrarian portfolio returns even when each stock alone is not mean-reverting.

## Source-supported result or scope
Authors distinguish cross-autocovariance and nonsynchronous information diffusion from investor-overreaction explanations.

## Risks and limitations
Portfolio-level reversal may mask directional lead-lag rather than single-name reversal; sample selection, small-stock tradability and costs matter.

## Required data for replication
Synchronized survivorship-safe stock returns, liquidity data, accurate trading calendar and out-of-sample covariance fit.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Decompose contrarian PnL into own-asset reversal vs cross-asset lead-lag contribution before considering any reversion model.
