# PAPER-065 — Bad News Travels Slowly: Size, Analyst Coverage, and the Profitability of Momentum Strategies

**Authors:** Harrison Hong; Terence Lim; Jeremy C. Stein  
**Publication:** The Journal of Finance 55(1), 265–295 (2000)  
**Primary research source:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/0022-1082.00206
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00206

> **Evidence classification: peer-reviewed publication or conference source record and abstract reviewed, not a full-text audit.** These are summaries of the authors' claims and contributions, not strategy endorsements. No new executable strategy, live results or independently verified historical returns.

## Mechanism / mathematical idea
Tests gradual company-information diffusion as an explanation for medium-horizon equity return continuation.

## Author-reported finding or theoretical scope
Momentum effectiveness declines with size beyond the smallest firms and is stronger in low-analyst-coverage names, especially past losers.

## Required data / feasibility gate
Historical stocks with dated analyst-coverage availability, firm size and industry controls, returns and borrow costs.

## Critique / potential failure
Analyst coverage is not a direct measure of informed order flow; untradeable small caps and short-side restrictions can dominate the signal.

## Falsifiable follow-up (proposal only)
Pre-specify low/high coverage sorts and evaluate incremental forward momentum returns at fixed firm size and liquidity controls.
