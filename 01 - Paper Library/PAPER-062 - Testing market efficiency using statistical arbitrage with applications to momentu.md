# PAPER-062 — Testing market efficiency using statistical arbitrage with applications to momentum and value strategies

**Authors:** Steve Hogan; Robert Jarrow; Melvyn Teo; Mitch Warachka  
**Publication:** Journal of Financial Economics 73(3), 525–565 (2004)  
**Primary research source:** https://digitalcommons.chapman.edu/business_articles/123/
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2003.10.004

> **Evidence classification: peer-reviewed publication or conference source record and abstract reviewed, not a full-text audit.** These are summaries of the authors' claims and contributions, not strategy endorsements. No new executable strategy, live results or independently verified historical returns.

## Mechanism / mathematical idea
Defines a formal statistical-arbitrage test based on evolving incremental profit and declining long-run uncertainty, then applies it to momentum and value portfolios.

## Author-reported finding or theoretical scope
Authors report statistically supported historical opportunities for some momentum/value constructions, controlling for a range of trading frictions in 1965–2000 data.

## Required data / feasibility gate
CRSP-style survivorship-safe stocks, incremental strategy PnL, short borrow, margin, market impact and candidate-family disclosure.

## Critique / potential failure
An asymptotic statistical-arbitrage designation is not a risk-free trade for a finite investor; profitability from 1965–2000 is not an OOS 2026 result.

## Falsifiable follow-up (proposal only)
Replicate the statistical test on frozen post-publication momentum and value signals, with multiple-testing correction and exact short financing.

1. Obtain full published paper; preserve version and check formula definitions, all timing and data sources.
