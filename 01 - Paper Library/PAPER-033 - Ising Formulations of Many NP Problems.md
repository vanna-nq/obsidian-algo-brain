# PAPER-033 — Ising Formulations of Many NP Problems

**Authors:** Andrew Lucas  
**Publication:** Frontiers in Physics 2, article 5 (2014)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fphy.2014.00005  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Gives mappings of diverse combinatorial optimization problems to Ising spin systems with penalty energies.

## Source-supported result or scope
General computational encoding toolkit for discrete constraints; no evidence of market forecasting or trading profitability.

## Risks and limitations
Penalty scaling, variable count, dense couplings, solver exactness and sample precision can defeat practical optimization; physics analogies do not imply alpha.

## Required data for replication
Well-defined integer/binary allocations, covariance/impact estimates, cardinality and lot constraints, classical baseline solvers.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Encode a capped-cardinality portfolio QUBO and benchmark classical MILP, simulated annealing, exact small cases against tradeoffs and solution quality.
