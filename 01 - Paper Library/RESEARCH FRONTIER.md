## Ranked first tests (not an instruction to test them together)
1. **PAPER-090 — Permutation entropy:** GC 5-minute; target future volatility or next-six-bar continuation; controls momentum and realized volatility. Very low data dependency and clear placebo.
2. **PAPER-088 — Time reversal:** GC 5-minute; compare past returns/variance feedback versus GARCH-null, and forward variance QLIKE. Requires strict causal diagnostics.
3. **PAPER-086 — Rough volatility:** use lagged GC/SPX500 volatility estimators; compare HAR-RV and EWMA under holdout QLIKE.
4. **PAPER-093 — Path signatures:** test a fixed signature degree on NVDA/SMH 5-minute plus comparably parameterized lag features.
5. **PAPER-092 — Recurrence structure:** frozen recurrence features for tail events; protect against hindsight crash labels.
6. **PAPER-103/104 — Glassy market displacement:** prove any two-timescale displacement anomaly survives intraday seasonality adjustments before claiming it models a market state.

## Missing datasets and non-replication boundaries
- **Full order-book/tick events** are required for legitimate PAPER-094, PAPER-097 and PAPER-102 studies. OHLCV aggregation is not a substitute.
- **Large synchronous instrument baskets** are required for PAPER-095 and PAPER-096; NVDA and SMH alone are inadequate for high-dimensional random matrix eigenvalue tests.
- **Multiple US equity indices** are needed to replicate PAPER-091 exactly; using gold or two ETFs is an independent adaptation, not replication.
- **PAPER-099/100** are agent-simulation mechanisms. Matching stylized facts is not proof that agents in real markets act this way.
- **PAPER-098** and crash alarms suffer from few independent observations and huge selection degrees of freedom.

## Audit policy
Source-screening vs full-method audit vs replicated outputs vs independently validated strategy remain separate statuses. Hypothesis tests require precommitted stop conditions and all rejected ideas stay archived. No published author performance numbers are our results.
