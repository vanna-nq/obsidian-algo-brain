# PAPER-028 — Identifying Bull and Bear Markets in Stock Returns

**Authors:** John M. Maheu; Thomas H. McCurdy  
**Publication:** Journal of Business & Economic Statistics 18(1), 100–112 (2000)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/07350015.2000.10524851  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Introduces duration-dependent Markov-switching return and conditional-variance states for bull/bear market analysis.

## Source-supported result or scope
Authors' model filters more than 160 years of monthly equity data into relatively stable-high-return and volatile-low-return states.

## Risks and limitations
Smoothed ex-post regimes use future data; fitted state names and transition probabilities may be unstable, especially when moved to intraday charts.

## Required data for replication
Long monthly return records, no hindsight backfills, filtered probabilities available before decisions, rolling estimation.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Measure whether ex-ante filtered, duration-sensitive states add net value to risk sizing beyond historical volatility alone.
