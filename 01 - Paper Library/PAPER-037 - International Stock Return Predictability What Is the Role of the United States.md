# PAPER-037 — International Stock Return Predictability What Is the Role of the United States

**Authors:** David E. Rapach; Jack K. Strauss; Guofu Zhou  
**Publication:** Journal of Finance 68(4), 1633–1662 (2013)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12041  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Tests cross-country monthly return lead-lag relationships and slow international information diffusion from US stock return innovations.

## Source-supported result or scope
Authors find lagged US market returns forecast returns in several non-US developed country markets more than reverse lead-lags.

## Risks and limitations
Monthly-country-level diffusion doesn't imply minute-scale lead-lag or FX-neutral free arbitrage; time-zone/calendar overlap and macro revisions matter.

## Required data for replication
Local-currency and USD monthly country stock returns, point-in-time FX, investable country ETFs/futures and market closure calendars.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Compare fixed lagged-US versus reverse cross-country signals on held-out post-publication periods with FX/market execution costs.
