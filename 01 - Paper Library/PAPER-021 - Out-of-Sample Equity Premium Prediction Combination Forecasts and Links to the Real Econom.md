# PAPER-021 — Out-of-Sample Equity Premium Prediction: Combination Forecasts and Links to the Real Economy

**Authors:** David E. Rapach; Jack K. Strauss; Guofu Zhou  
**Publication:** Review of Financial Studies 23(2), 821–862 (2010)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhp063  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Combines forecasts from multiple economic predictors to reduce model uncertainty and prediction variability.

## Source-supported result or scope
Authors report more consistent OOS aggregate equity-premium forecasting gains than individual unstable predictor models.

## Risks and limitations
Macro release dates and revisions can create lookahead; forecast R² improvements do not automatically imply daily trading profitability.

## Required data for replication
Vintage macro predictor time series, daily/monthly return horizon mappings, real-time release dates.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Compare precommitted equal-weighted forecast ensemble to regularized baseline on post-publication monthly equity-premium forecasts.
