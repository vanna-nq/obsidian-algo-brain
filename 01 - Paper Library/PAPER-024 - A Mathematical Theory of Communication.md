# PAPER-024 — A Mathematical Theory of Communication

**Authors:** Claude E. Shannon  
**Publication:** Bell System Technical Journal 27(3), 379–423; 27(4), 623–656 (1948)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Defines core communication-theory quantities including entropy and channel capacity as a measure of reduction in uncertainty.

## Source-supported result or scope
Foundational mathematical information theory, NOT an empirical financial-market study and NOT a formula for profitability.

## Risks and limitations
Low estimated entropy alone does not guarantee tradable predictability; finite-sample entropy bias and temporal dependence matter.

## Required data for replication
Causal forecast distributions, clearly specified symbol alphabets/labels and sufficient independent observations.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Show whether a rigorously estimated conditional information increment relates to net after-cost decision utility on held-out futures.
