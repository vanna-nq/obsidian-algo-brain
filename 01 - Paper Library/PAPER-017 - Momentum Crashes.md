# PAPER-017 — Momentum Crashes

**Authors:** Kent Daniel; Tobias J. Moskowitz  
**Publication:** Journal of Financial Economics 122(2), 221–247 (2016)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2015.12.002  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Studies crash risk of cross-sectional momentum portfolios, especially after market declines and amid rebounds.

## Source-supported result or scope
Authors find momentum crashes are partly predictable in panic and high-volatility states and develop a conditional momentum strategy.

## Risks and limitations
Momentum crash findings are not directly transferable to intraday SPY trend-following; strategy universe and lookback differ.

## Required data for replication
Survivorship-safe winners/losers stocks, rolling volatility/bear-market state, financing and short costs.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Predefine panic exposure-control rule and test whether it reduces left-tail risk net of costs on untouched post-publication data.
