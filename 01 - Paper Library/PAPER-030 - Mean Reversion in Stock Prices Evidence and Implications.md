# PAPER-030 — Mean Reversion in Stock Prices Evidence and Implications

**Authors:** James M. Poterba; Lawrence H. Summers  
**Publication:** Journal of Financial Economics 22(1), 27–59 (1988)  
**Verified source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-405X(88)90021-9  
**Evidence level:** Publication metadata and publisher / author abstract or accessible summary screened. Full-text derivations, exact formulas and study tables have **NOT** been independently audited here.

## Core contribution / mathematical mechanism
Examines statistical evidence for transitory stock-price components and return autocorrelation across short and long horizons.

## Source-supported result or scope
Authors report short-horizon positive and longer-horizon negative point autocorrelation estimates but do NOT reject the random walk conventionally at all examined levels.

## Risks and limitations
Long-run sample and low-powered inference do not justify reliable minute-scale OU dynamics, nor does negative autocorrelation guarantee executable profit.

## Required data for replication
Very long equity index return histories, consistent dividend adjustments, stationarity tests and horizons matched to original claim.

## Falsifiable follow-up experiment (proposed only)
Pre-register horizon-specific variance-ratio tests and check whether negative serial dependence remains in recent unseen samples after uncertainty correction.
